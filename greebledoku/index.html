Greebledoku

How to play

Find all the Greebles. There is one per color section. They never share rows or columns or diagnol paths. When one is found, cross out the rest of its color, row, column, and diagnol path.

Tap or drag to cross out paths.
Double tap to guess where a Greeble is.
