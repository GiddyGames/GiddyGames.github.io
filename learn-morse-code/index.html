How to play

1) Press the top Play button to receive a transmission. Choose what level of difficulty you want to play at the bottom of the screen. Choose what challenge mode you want the transmission in: No visual (🚫), Dots and Dashes (👁), or Letters (🔤).

2) Decode the message below with the Beeper Button — tap for a dot, hold for a dash. Copy the transmission's code.

3) Tap Verify to see if you got it right.

Bonus) At Level 3 and 4, once you Verify a transmission correctly, respond with a shiritori — starting your word with the last letter of the transmission's word. Example: Red→Dry, Yell→Lot, Toll→Lad, Dew→Won. A rhyme works too, but shiritori is the main way to respond.
